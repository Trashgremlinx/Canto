Before you start10 things to know about this beta
  • This is a betaSome things may not work quite right yet. When something looks off, please tell us. That’s the whole point.
  • Save your lore file oftenCanto keeps your lore in this browser only. If you clear your browser data or switch computers, unsaved changes are gone. Use Save my lore file in Files when you’re done for the day.
  • Installed 0.1.0? Install once moreVersions before 0.1.1 can’t update themselves. Click the install link on Canto’s page one more time, and from then on updates arrive on their own. Your lore won’t be touched.
  • Only on your own pagesYour words and Canto’s card show on your den and your own wolves, not on other players’ pages. That’s on purpose. Studs you borrow go under Outside studs.
  • Open the Family page firstWolvden only shows a pup’s parents on its Family page. Visit it once and Canto can suggest the pup’s family, as long as one parent is in your lore.
  • Counting years by a wolfIf you chose to count years by one wolf’s age, visit that wolf now and then so Canto can see how old it is.
  • One pack at a timeYou can load as many packs as you like, but only the one you’ve picked at the top of Canto is shown.
  • You need TwillCanto works alongside Twill, so keep Twill installed and switched on.
  • Fonts and picturesCanto loads its two fonts from Google Fonts. Pictures in your Tales load from wherever you host them. Nothing else leaves your browser.
  • Tales live in your lore fileYour writing is saved in this browser as you type. Use Save my lore file to keep a copy, just like the rest of your lore.

Thank you for testing Canto. It lets your pack’s own lore show up on Wolvden: your words, your bloodlines, your calendar and the names you’ve saved for pups. You write it once in a lore file, or build it inside Canto, and it appears on the pages you already use.

This guide shows you what to look for, what to watch out for, and what’s changed. Nothing here can affect your account: Canto only reads the page you have open.

Release notes

0.1.825 October 2026the latest version
  • New
    Getting startedEach part of Canto now has a short checklist that walks you through it. Steps tick themselves off as you do them. Finished ones fold into Getting started under the “?”, where you can open them again any time.
  • New
    Did you know?A short tip at the top of each section, a different one each visit. Close one, or switch them all off under the “?”.
  • New
    WalkthroughsThe first time you open Canto, set up the calendar, make a line, write a tale or see your lines on your den, a few bubbles show you around. Skip any of them, or run them again under the “?”.
0.1.725 October 2026
  • Updated
    A new way around CantoCanto now opens on a list of its sections, each with a line saying what’s inside, like Twill’s hub. Step into one, hop to another from the row along the top, and ‹ takes you back. Canto remembers where you were.
  • Updated
    Help is one tap awayHelp & About moved to the blue “?” beside the ×, on every screen. Tap it again to go back to where you were.
  • Fixed
    Tabs cut off at the topOn some screens the tabs at the top of Canto were clipped. They’re gone now, replaced by the new list, and Canto is a little wider so everything fits.
  • Fixed
    Links on custom densOn dens with a custom layout, Canto’s link buttons picked up the den’s own link style. They keep Canto’s look now.
0.1.624 October 2026
  • Fixed
    The calendar on ExploreOn Wolvden’s Explore page, the calendar wheel picked up the Explore map as its background. It stays clean now.
0.1.524 October 2026
  • New
    TalesA place to write your pack’s lore, with bold, italic, headings, lists and pictures. “The pack” comes first, then a page for every bloodline and family, and you can add your own. Find it in the new Tales tab, or in the Tales box at the top of your den.
  • New
    Need a nudge?Starting from nothing? Tap “nudge” for questions about your pack’s land, hunting, leaders, beliefs, history and more. Each bloodline page gets its own questions too.
  • New
    ProjectsA line can now be a bloodline, a breeding project, or both. A wolf keeps one bloodline and can be in as many projects as you like. The Show bar can switch between bloodlines and projects.
  • New
    Use your Twill TrayPut wolves in Twill’s Tray, then give them all a line or a project at once from the Bloodlines tab. Wolves that already have a line are asked about first.
  • New
    Wolves by lineThe Bloodlines tab lists every wolf under its line, family or project. On your den, it also shows which wolves aren’t in a line yet.
  • Updated
    Easier first stepsA short note on the Bloodlines tab shows how to give a wolf a line. Den look now says what it does.
  • Fixed
    Changing a line’s kindTurning a bloodline into a project now moves its wolves into the project, and the other way round, so nobody is left half in.
  • Fixed
    Removing a lineRemoving a line or family now asks first if any wolves have it, and takes it off them too.
  • Updated
    Colours you can seePicking a very dark or very pale colour for a line now warns you it may vanish on Wolvden’s dark or light mode, and offers a better shade.
0.1.423 October 2026
  • New
    Den lookPick how lines show on your den: Glow, Soft, Thin edge, Name bar, Stripe, Corner, Corner tabs, Corner brackets, Badge or Off. With Corner tabs and Corner brackets, each colour sits in its own corner of the card. Sliders set the thickness, glow and brightness, and you can choose the dot beside a line’s name. Find it at the bottom of the Bloodlines tab. It’s kept in your browser, not your lore file.
  • New
    Outside studsBorrowed a stud from another player? Add him on the Bloodlines tab with his line and wolf ID. His pups then show both parents’ lines, with a small “outside” tag on him.
  • New
    Quiet denTurn on “Only show lines while one is picked” and your den stays plain until you pick a line in the Show bar.
  • Updated
    Every side of a familyFamilies made from other families now show up to four colours. Each parent takes half the ring, and a parent that’s a family shares its half between its own two lines. So Emberfall (Hollow × Ashen) with Thornmere (Briar × Reed) shows all four.
  • New
    The whole tree on hoverHover or tap a family made from families to see each parent and the lines it came from.
  • Updated
    A lighter fadePicking a line in the Show bar now fades the other wolves a little instead of turning them grey. It’s much lighter on phones and older computers, and you can set how much they fade.
  • Updated
    Your own wolves onlyCanto’s card now shows on your own wolves only. Other players’ wolves are left alone; use Outside studs for the ones you breed with.
  • Updated
    Starting the calendar overYour season names are kept and shown in step 3, so you can change them, or go back to Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter with one link.
0.1.323 October 2026
  • New
    Help & AboutA new tab in Canto. Report a problem, ask a question or suggest an idea: each opens the form on this page, already filled in with Canto, your version and the page you were on. Plus links to learn more, and everything about Canto, like Twill’s About.
0.1.222 October 2026
  • Fixed
    One mark per eventThe calendar wheel showed two dots for an event set to early, mid or late in a season, and six for a whole season. Now each event is a single mark: a dot for one day, a band across the days it covers for anything longer. Events that overlap sit side by side so neither hides the other.

  • New
