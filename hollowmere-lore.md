## Tales

### The pack
Hollowmere keeps to the **Shallows**, the still water below the falls, where the mist never *quite* lifts. Pups learn to fish before they learn to hunt.

#### Who leads
The alpha is chosen at the *Ember Walk*, when the last of Ashfall burns low. Any wolf may stand, but only one walks the whole ridge before dawn.

#### What they keep
- the Ember Walk, every late Ashfall
- the First Frost Howl, on the first day of Deepcold
- the names of every alpha, kept by the Storykeeper

### Hollow
The first wolves of the valley. Hollow wolves are quiet, patient fishers, and the first to hear the river change.
