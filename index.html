Field Guide: Canto, a tester introduction

What is Canto?

Every pack has its tales. Canto lets you write yours in one file and see it on Wolvden: your own words softly underlined with their meanings, your bloodlines glowing on your den, families named when two lines meet, a calendar in your own season names, and the names you’ve saved for pups waiting beside the rename box.

It sits on top of Twill, so you need Twill installed first.

Thank you for testing it. This page gets you set up, gives you a list of things to try, and says how to send what you find.

Set up steps

  1. Have Twill working

    Canto is an add-on, so install Twill first if you haven’t. Its stag should be in the corner of every Wolvden page.

  2. Click the install link

    With Tampermonkey in place, this one link is the whole install:

    Install Canto

    Tampermonkey shows you the script with an Install button. Press it, then reload Wolvden. A small campfire sits just above Twill’s stag. Click it, or press Alt+C.

    If the link opens as a wall of text instead, Tampermonkey did not catch it. Select all of it, open the Tampermonkey dashboard, click the + tab, delete the starter script, paste, and save with Ctrl+S.
  3. Load some lore

    Open the hub and go to Files. Load your own lore file, start a new pack and build it inside Canto, or try the sample pack below.

Updating later

Nothing to do. Tampermonkey checks for a new version on its own and updates you, the same as Twill. Everything you’ve written stays where it is. If you installed version 0.1.0, click the install link once more: from 0.1.1 on, Canto keeps itself up to date.

The sample pack

Hollowmere is a made-up pack for trying things out. Download it, or copy it into a plain text file saved as hollowmere-lore.md, and load it in Files. Its wolf IDs won’t match yours, so give a few of your own wolves its lines from their pages.

Sending feedback

Post in #beta-test, under the Twill category on the Society of Fur Discord, or message Fallowe directly. For anything that looks broken, please include:

  • WhereWhich page: your den, a wolf, a Family page, or a tab in the hub.
  • What you didThe steps, in order, so it can be repeated.
  • What you expectedAnd what happened instead.
  • A screenshotOf the page, with the hub open if it’s involved.
  • Your setupBrowser, and the Twill and Canto versions. Canto’s is at the bottom of Files.
  • Your lore fileOnly if you’re happy to share it. It often explains a lot.

Ideas and wishes are just as welcome as bugs. Say what you were trying to do, not only what you wanted Canto to have.

Questions

Where do my words show?

Only on your own pages: your den and your own wolves. Other people’s pages stay as they wrote them.

Why doesn’t a pup’s card suggest its family?

Wolvden only shows a wolf’s parents on its Family page. Open that once and Canto remembers who the parents are, then offers the family there and on the pup’s own card.

My year isn’t moving.

If you count years by one wolf’s age, Canto reads that age from the wolf’s page, because rollovers are your own. Open that wolf now and then. Counting by Wolvden’s seasons moves on its own.

Can I keep more than one pack?

Yes. Load as many files as you like and swap between them at the top of the hub. One is in use at a time.

What isn’t in this version yet?

Sub-lines within a line, wolf birthdays on the calendar, and names tied to a sex or season.

What it will not do

  • It never loads a page for you, and never refreshes anything on a timer.
  • It never clicks, submits or fills a form for you. Names waiting are copied for you to paste yourself.
  • It sends nothing anywhere. No server, no account, no tracking. Its two fonts come from Google Fonts.
  • It does not gather up the site. It keeps what you write, plus the parent IDs of wolves whose Family page you open and the age of one wolf if you count years by it.
